## 📞 Phase 1: Discovery Call Script

### Opening (5 minutes)

"Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time to chat today. I'm excited to learn more about your business and see if CreatorOS is a good fit for your social media needs.

Before we dive into the demo, I'd love to understand a bit about what you're currently doing for social media. Sound good?"

### Discovery Questions (10-15 minutes)

**About their business:**
1. "Tell me about your business - what do you do?"
2. "Who is your target audience?"
3. "What are your main business goals for this year?"

**About their social media:**
1. "Which social media platforms are you currently active on?"
   - Note: Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, TikTok, etc.

2. "How much time are you or your team spending on social media each week?"
   - Note: This helps calculate ROI/time savings

3. "What are your biggest challenges with social media right now?"
   - Common answers: Time-consuming, inconsistent posting, poor results, managing multiple platforms, team collaboration

4. "Are you currently using any social media management tools?"
   - Note: Competitors they might be comparing you to

5. "How many people on your team post to social media?"
   - Note: Determines pricing tier

**About their goals:**
1. "What would success look like for you with social media in 6 months?"
2. "What kind of results are you hoping to see? (followers, engagement, sales, etc.)"

### Presenting the Solution (15-20 minutes)

"Based on what you've shared, let me show you how CreatorOS can help..."

**Demo checklist:**
- [ ] Show dashboard (analytics, overview)
- [ ] Demo posting to multiple platforms at once
- [ ] Show DM management (if they mentioned this pain point)
- [ ] Show content calendar
- [ ] Show analytics and reporting
- [ ] Demonstrate mobile responsiveness (if applicable)

**Key benefits to emphasize:**
- ⏰ **Time savings**: "You mentioned spending 10 hours/week on social - this could cut that to 2-3 hours"
- 💰 **Cost savings**: "Compared to [competitor] at $X/month, you save $Y annually"
- 📊 **Better results**: "Our analytics help you post at optimal times, improving engagement"
- 👥 **Team collaboration**: "Your whole team can collaborate from one dashboard"

### Handling Objections (5-10 minutes)

See PRICING_STRATEGY.md for objection handling scripts.

### Closing (5 minutes)

**Soft close:**
"Based on what we've discussed, I think the [Starter/Professional/Agency] tier would be perfect for you. It includes [list key features]. Does that sound like it would meet your needs?"

**Next steps:**
"Great! Here's what happens next:
1. I'll send you a proposal with pricing and contract by end of day
2. Once you review and sign, we'll schedule your onboarding session
3. We'll get you up and running within 48 hours
4. You'll start saving time and seeing results immediately

Do you have any other questions for me?"

## 🎓 Phase 3: Onboarding Session Guide

### Session Structure (60-90 minutes)

**Introduction (5 minutes)**
- Welcome and agenda overview
- Set expectations

**Account Setup (15 minutes)**
- Show them how to log in
- Tour of the dashboard
- Connect first social media account (walk through step-by-step)
- Have them connect remaining accounts

**Core Features Training (30-40 minutes)**

1. **Creating a Post (10 minutes)**
   - Click "New Post"
   - Write content
   - Add images/video
   - Select platforms
   - Schedule vs. post now
   - Have THEM create a test post

2. **Content Calendar (5 minutes)**
   - Show calendar view
   - How to reschedule posts
   - Planning ahead

3. **Analytics Dashboard (10 minutes)**
   - Overview of metrics
   - Follower growth
   - Engagement rates
   - Best performing posts
   - How to use this data to improve

4. **DM Management (10 minutes)**
   - Accessing messages
   - Responding to DMs
   - Managing conversations
   - Notification settings

5. **Team Collaboration (5 minutes, if applicable)**
   - Adding team members
   - Setting roles/permissions
   - Collaboration features

**Q&A and Next Steps (10-15 minutes)**
- Answer their questions
- Set goals for first 30 days
- Schedule follow-up check-in
- Provide support contact info

## 💰 Upselling During Onboarding

### When to Upsell

**During initial call** (if they're clearly underbuying):
"Based on what you've told me about your team size and goals, I think the Professional tier would actually serve you better. Here's why..."

**At 30-day review** (when they see results):
"You've grown so much this month! You're almost hitting the limits of your Starter plan. Want to upgrade to Professional to unlock [features] and handle this growth?"

**When they hit limits:**
Set up automated emails:
- "You've connected 4 of your 5 allowed accounts. Need more? Upgrade to Professional for 15 accounts!"
- "You have 2 of 3 team members. Adding more? Professional plan includes up to 10 team members."

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: Client can't connect Instagram account

**Solution:**
- Instagram requires Facebook Business Manager
- Walk them through: [detailed steps]
- Or: "Let me screen share and we'll do it together"

### Issue: Team member didn't receive invitation

**Solution:**
- Check spam folder
- Resend invitation
- Manually create account and send login details

### Issue: Posts not going through

**Solution:**
- Check social account is still connected
- Verify platform API status (sometimes Facebook/Instagram have outages)
- Check post content meets platform requirements (character limits, image sizes)
- Reconnect account if needed

### Issue: "This is too complicated"

**Solution:**
- Schedule extra training session (free)
- Create video tutorials specific to their use case
- Offer to manage first month with them (extra fee)
