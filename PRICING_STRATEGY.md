## 💡 Pro Tips for Maximum Revenue

### 1. Start with Higher Prices
- It's easier to discount than to raise prices
- Higher price = perceived higher value
- You can always run promotions

### 2. Annual Contracts
- Offer 10-20% discount for annual pre-pay
- Guaranteed revenue for the year
- Lower churn

### 3. Upsell Path
- Start client on Starter tier
- After 2-3 months, show usage stats
- "You're almost at your limit - upgrade to Professional and get [benefits]"

### 4. Bundle Services
- CreatorOS ($299/month) + Content Creation ($500/month) = $799/month
- You keep CreatorOS fee, outsource content creation for $300-400, pocket $200-300 difference

### 5. White-Label for Agencies
- Charge agencies $1,000-2,000/month
- They rebrand as their own tool
- They charge THEIR clients $200-500/month per client
- Win-win: They make profit, you get guaranteed high-value customer
